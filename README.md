⚔️ CRUSADER'S MOD | Minecraft Bedrock

A massive Bedrock addon packed with custom mechanics, machines, events, pets, abilities and more.

🌿 SEASONS
The world changes every 5 Minecraft days:
• Spring: Normal gameplay
• Summer: Speed III + Jump Boost + Hunger
• Monsoon: Haste II + Weakness + thunderstorms
• Autumn: Strength II + Slowness
• Winter: Slowness II + Saturation III

⚰️ SOUL CHEST
When you die, your items are sealed inside a chest at your death location. Only you can open it, and it disappears after 2 minutes.
Enable with /tag @s add keepgrave

🎲 RANDOM CLASS KITS
New players are randomly assigned one of 6 classes:
Archer, Soldier, Sorcerer, Imposter, Magician or Engineer.

🌳 TREE CAPITATOR
Break an entire tree with one axe swing. Toggle it by sneaking and using the axe.

🪝 GRAPPLING HOOK
Name a sword "grapple" to fire a leashed arrow that pulls you toward where it lands.

🎰 CASINO
Bet 32 diamonds for a chance to win diamonds, emeralds, gold or Totems of Undying.

⏳ AGING SYSTEM
Age 1 year every Minecraft day. Your abilities change as you age, and reaching age 150 rewards you with an Elytra.

🍎 SPECIAL EVENTS
• Day 67: Chance for Golden Apple rain and a rare Notch's Apple.
• Notch's Match: A rare daily event featuring Phantom riders that may bring loot.

🛒 OVERHAULED TRADES
Fletchers, Shepherds, Stone Masons, Clerics and Wandering Traders have expanded trades.

⚙️ CUSTOM REDSTONE

Impulse Block
Pushes nearby entities away. Redstone strength controls the force.

Inverse Impulse Block
Pulls entities from up to 20 blocks away. Players are ignored.

Block Placer
A 15-mode redstone machine:
• 1-6: Place blocks
• 7-12: Break blocks
• 13: Special conversion
• 14: Load a saved block
• 15: Save and remove a block

Entity Teleporter
Teleports entities upward. Redstone strength determines the distance.

🛠️ EXTRA FEATURES

• Pick up mobs with a Golden Hoe
• Pick up blocks with a Netherite Hoe while preserving their data
• Create bombs using items or boats named "bomb"
• Detonate bombs with a Compass or Stone Button
• Built-in WorldEdit for admins
• Quick equipment management

🐝 PETS

Bone Drone
• Tame with Bones
• Flies and hovers
• Can be ridden and controlled
• Supports different ammunition
• Attacks monsters automatically

Calcite Turret
• Tame with Calcite
• Follows you while standing
• Can be set to stay in place
• Automatically attacks monsters

✨ ABILITIES & ITEMS

Crusader Shell
Shoot it from a bow and sneak while holding a Shell in your offhand to teleport to the arrow.

Teleport Book
Teleports you and nearby entities to the nearest player.

Teleporter Book
Teleports you to the farthest player on the server.

The addon includes an in-game menu where you can explore Features, Custom Blocks, Pets, Abilities, Items, Changelog and Experimental features.
