CrusadersBedrock

Custom survival mechanics, redstone machines, pets, abilities, items, seasonal events, and experimental utilities, built for Minecraft Bedrock Edition.

I'm a passionate Bedrock modder who enjoys turning ideas into actual gameplay experiences. I'm especially interested in creating custom mechanics, items, blocks, abilities, mobs, systems, and experimental features that expand what the game can do.

Features

CrusadersBedrock features split into two flavors: vanilla items and blocks given extra purpose, and brand new custom systems built from scratch.

§02.1

Vanilla+

Ordinary items and blocks, given extra purpose.

Teleport Book

A book named "Teleport". Throw it to teleport the user and nearby entities (radius 3 blocks) to the farthest player.

Teleporter Book

A book named "Teleporter". Throw it to teleport the farthest players to you.

Waypoint

Teleport Scroll

Bind Paper to a Lodestone to create a scroll that teleports you back to that exact spot.

How to Bind

  1. Place a Lodestone.
  2. Hold Paper and use it on the Lodestone.
  3. The space directly above the Lodestone must be clear.
  4. The Paper is consumed and a Teleport Scroll is created, bound to that spot.

How to Use

  1. Use the Teleport Scroll.
  2. You're teleported to the bound Lodestone.
  3. The scroll is consumed after use.
Note: each scroll holds one destination. Bind a new one for a different waypoint.

Potion Making

Brewing

Custom Potion Maker

Brew powerful custom potions beyond vanilla Minecraft.

How to Make One

  1. Cut normal Obsidian in a stonecutter to get GlowingObsidian.
  2. Place the GlowingObsidian block down.
  3. Hold a Potion, Splash Potion, or Wooden Sword and interact with the block. The Effect Binder UI opens.
  4. Choose Add Effect, pick an effect, amplifier, and duration.
  5. Confirm and pay the Glowstone Dust cost to bind it to the item.
  6. Drink the potion, throw the splash potion, or hit with the sword to trigger the bound effects.
Note: each item can hold up to 10 bound effects, and cost scales with amplifier and duration.

Minigame

Slot Machine

A casino-style minigame that rewards lucky players with valuable loot.

How it Works

  1. Use a Diamond to activate the Slot Machine.
  2. Each spin costs 32 Diamonds.
  3. Three animated slots spin with sound effects.
  4. Match all three symbols to win a jackpot.
